How can we help?

Panezo helps you browse two sources, take local notes, compare readable text and save selected research. It displays websites or its own native notes; it does not embed other installed iPhone apps. Website login, attachments, video and reader compatibility vary. Compare is deterministic text comparison, not an AI recommendation or live price feed.

Contact Ilyas Akbar Almadani: ilyasaalmadani@gmail.com. Include the version/build shown in Panezo > Settings, iOS version, steps and a redacted screenshot. Never include passwords or private notes. Export important notes/research before troubleshooting. Do not uninstall or reset Panezo to update it: local data can be lost on removal.

A failed Research save can be retried after Reload saved research without clearing your writing. A failed draft save remains only in the current session until Retry succeeds. If a website stops responding after process loss, use its pane Reconnect action. Clear website data signs out sites and is not a routine troubleshooting step.
