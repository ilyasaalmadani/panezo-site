Privacy policy

Effective 23 September 2026

Panezo stores your work on your device. The current app has no developer analytics, advertising SDK or developer-operated content-upload service. Browsing connects to the websites you choose; this is not anonymous browsing.

Last page addresses, layouts, Scratch notes, saved Workspaces, Notebook notes, selected Research findings and user-written Research drafts are local. Device backups may include app data. Recovery saves user-written titles/annotations; it does not automatically save temporary extracted website findings.

Compare reads limited readable page text when requested. Form values and embedded documents are excluded by the extractor. Only selected findings explicitly saved by you are retained in Research or Notes. Sharing/export sends content to destinations you choose, under their policies.

The browser panes share website storage, including cookies and sessions. Websites receive normal browsing requests and apply their own policies. Panezo is not affiliated with visited websites.

Websites may request access to the camera, microphone, or a photo you choose. Review each iOS permission prompt. Panezo does not request these permissions on launch. Background listening uses real user-started media and depends on the website and iOS.

HTTP authentication credentials may be reused during the app session. Remember securely stores them in this device's Keychain with access while unlocked; these saved items do not sync through iCloud Keychain. Credentials are provided to the website being authenticated, not a Panezo server.

Use each note/workspace/Research record's delete action to remove it. Use Discard draft in the Research editor to remove writing recovery. Clear website data removes cookies/cache/storage in both panes. Forget saved website credentials separately removes Panezo's remembered HTTP credentials and temporary cache; existing website logins may still require signing out on the website.

Deleted data may remain in older backups or exported copies. Manage those through their provider. Manage website accounts/server data through that website. Updating Panezo does not intentionally clear your data.

Contact: ilyasaalmadani@gmail.com. Email follows your mail provider's policies. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or private page content. Changes to this policy will be reflected in its effective date.
