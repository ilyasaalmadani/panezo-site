Two websites. One iPhone screen.

Panezo is a split screen browser with notes. Read one page, write or compare with another, and keep a floating mini browser nearby while audio keeps playing.

Download on the App Store

Free to download for iPhone. Also on Android (Google Play). Panezo Pro is an optional subscription.

What is new in 1.3.0

  • Tabs in each pane (Pro): keep several pages open above and below.
  • Floating mini browser (Pro): move it, resize it, make it full screen, or shrink it to a bubble while audio keeps playing.
  • Liquid Glass look and four themes: Automatic, Panezo, Light and Dark (free).
  • Page summaries and questions with Apple Intelligence on the device, where available. Free: 3 summaries. Pro: unlimited.
  • One-page mode with a bottom address bar, for one-handed reading (free).
  • Clean pages (Pro): hides web ads and cookie notices.
  • Downloads list, private panes and a Face ID lock.

Built for reading, noting and comparing

  • Two websites on one screen, above and below, with a resizable split
  • Notes in each pane and a searchable Notebook
  • Saved pairs that restore both pages and the layout
  • On-device comparison of readable page text, with selected findings saved to Research

Free and Pro

Free includes two panes, 2 saved pairs, 3 notes and 3 Compare runs, plus themes, one-page mode, downloads, private panes and Face ID lock. Panezo Pro on iPhone is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, bought through the App Store; prices are shown in the App Store. On Android, Panezo Pro is a monthly subscription on Google Play and adds unlimited Workspaces and Notebook notes, export, Compare and the Research library. Your notes and workspaces stay on your device, and there is no Panezo account.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a split screen browser for iPhone?

Yes. Panezo shows two web pages on one iPhone screen, one above the other, with a split you can resize. Each pane has its own tabs (Pro), and you can take notes next to what you read.

How do I open two websites at once on iPhone?

Install Panezo, open it, and load one site in the top pane and another in the bottom pane. Drag the divider to give either page more room. You can save the pair as a workspace and reopen it later.

Is there a picture-in-picture browser for iPhone?

Panezo Pro has a floating mini browser. Move it, resize it, make it full screen, or shrink it to a bubble while audio keeps playing, then bring it back whenever you want.

Can I watch a video while browsing on iPhone?

Yes. With the floating mini browser in Panezo Pro, a page can float above your other page while you keep reading or taking notes. Shrink it to a bubble and the audio keeps playing.

What is free and what needs Panezo Pro?

Free: two panes, 2 saved pairs, 3 notes, 3 Compare runs, 3 page summaries, themes (Automatic, Panezo, Light, Dark), one-page mode with a bottom address bar, a downloads list, private panes and Face ID lock. Pro adds tabs in each pane, the floating mini browser, Clean pages, unlimited summaries and higher limits.

How does Panezo Pro work on iPhone?

Panezo Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, bought through the App Store. Prices are shown in the App Store before you buy. You can cancel any time in Settings under your name, then Subscriptions.

Does Panezo summarize pages?

Yes, on devices where Apple Intelligence is available. You can ask for a page summary or ask a question about the page. It runs on your device. The free version includes 3 summaries; Pro is unlimited.

Does Panezo block ads and cookie notices?

Clean pages, a Pro feature, hides web ads and cookie notices on the pages you read so they are easier to follow.
