Make room for ideas.

Read. Note. Compare.

Panezo brings two websites and native notes into one focused iPhone workspace. Keep a source above, write below, compare readable text and save selected findings locally.

Free, no ads, no tracking

Panezo is free. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no Panezo account. Your notes, workspaces and research stay on your iPhone.

  • Two websites on one screen, above and below, with a resizable split
  • Scratch notes in each pane and a searchable Notebook
  • Saved workspaces that restore both pages and the layout
  • On-device comparison of readable page text, with selected findings saved to Research

About the developer

Panezo is designed and built by Ilyas Akbar Almadani, an independent developer. It is a small, careful app: native SwiftUI, system WebKit and no third-party code.
