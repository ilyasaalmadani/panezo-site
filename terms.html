Panezo terms of use

Effective 12 November 2026 · PT JIJI LABS DIGITAL

The app

Panezo is a two-pane web browser with local notes for iPhone and Android. The free version includes everyday browsing in two panes, favorites, history, downloads, saved Workspaces and Notebook notes within free limits (on iPhone: 2 saved pairs and 3 notes). Optional Panezo Pro subscriptions unlock more.

Panezo Pro (Android)

Panezo Pro is an optional monthly subscription sold through Google Play. It adds unlimited Workspaces and Notebook notes, export of notes and research, Compare and the Research library. The price is shown in Google Play before you buy, in your local currency.

The subscription renews automatically each month until you cancel. You can cancel anytime in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions; Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Payment, renewals and refunds are handled by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service and Google Play refund policies.

Panezo Pro (iPhone)

Panezo Pro on iPhone is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, bought through the App Store. It adds features such as tabs in each pane, the floating mini browser, Clean pages and higher limits. Prices are shown in the App Store before you buy.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. You can manage or cancel in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. You can restore a purchase with "Restore purchase" in Settings > Panezo Pro. Payment, renewals and refunds are handled by Apple. The Apple standard End User License Agreement applies to the app.

Websites you visit

Panezo shows websites chosen by you. Their content, accounts, subscriptions and privacy practices belong to those websites. Panezo is not affiliated with the websites you visit. Only compare, save or share content you are allowed to use.

Your data

Your notes, Workspaces, Research, favorites and history stay on your device. See the privacy policy. Uninstalling the app removes its local data; export what you want to keep first.
