The app

Panezo is a two-pane web browser with local notes for Android and iPhone. The free version includes everyday browsing in two panes, favorites, history, downloads, 2 saved Workspaces and 3 Notebook notes.

Panezo Pro (Android)

Panezo Pro is an optional monthly subscription sold through Google Play. It adds unlimited Workspaces and Notebook notes, export of notes and research, Compare and the Research library. The price is shown in Google Play before you buy, in your local currency.

The subscription renews automatically each month until you cancel. You can cancel anytime in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions; Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Payment, renewals and refunds are handled by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service and Google Play refund policies.

Websites you visit

Panezo shows websites chosen by you. Their content, accounts, subscriptions and privacy practices belong to those websites. Panezo is not affiliated with the websites you visit. Only compare, save or share content you are allowed to use.

Your data

Your notes, Workspaces, Research, favorites and history stay on your device. See the privacy policy. Uninstalling the app removes its local data; export what you want to keep first.
